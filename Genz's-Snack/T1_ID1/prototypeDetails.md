# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Health-conscious parents and young professionals seeking nutritious, convenient snacks with trusted ingredients and transparent sourcing, primarily shopping at mainstream grocery and natural foods retailers.

---

## Value Proposition

Launch a co-branded multipack snack bar that combines the familiar taste and mass appeal of Cheerios with Cascadian Farm's organic, wholesome ingredients. The product is designed for easy daily snacking and introduces the Cascadian Farm brand to new audiences by leveraging Cheerios' established shelf presence. Execution will feature interactive retail displays and public participation opportunities, aligning with constraints of low consumer effort and clear, measurable success. This approach ensures operational practicality and maximizes engagement without significant new production complexities.

---

## Business Impact

Drives incremental revenue by cross-leveraging two trusted brands, increasing basket size and trial among new customer segments. Expands Cascadian Farm's retail footprint, while Cheerios' brand equity accelerates product adoption. Clear metrics enable rapid iteration and retail partner alignment.
