# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Food industry professionals, culinary innovators, and engaged food enthusiasts interested in co-creating and influencing the next generation of flavors through collaborative, expert-led innovation sessions.

---

## Value Proposition

General Mills' Expert-Led Innovation Labs bring together chefs, food scientists, and passionate consumers in live, co-creation sessions to develop new flavors. Each session is guided by culinary experts and incorporates real-time public feedback to iteratively refine concepts. This open innovation model leverages professional expertise and consumer insight, ensuring new flavors are market-ready and relevant. Sessions are structured to balance expert input with accessible public participation, supporting scalable, data-driven flavor discovery without excessive cost or operational complexity.

---

## Business Impact

This model accelerates new flavor development cycles, increases consumer relevance, and supports cost-effective innovation by directly connecting expert ideation with real-time market feedback. The result is higher product adoption rates and a more agile response to evolving taste trends.
