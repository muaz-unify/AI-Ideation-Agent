# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Gen Z college students seeking convenient, tech-enabled access to limited-edition ice cream on campus.

---

## Value Proposition

General Mills introduces unattended, app-controlled smart freezer vending machines that dispense limited-edition ice cream directly to students. By leveraging mobile app integration, these units allow students to browse, purchase, and collect exclusive ice cream flavors without staff assistance. The solution addresses demand for convenience and novelty, reduces operational staffing requirements, and offers a frictionless, digitally native experience tailored to Gen Z's expectations for automation and instant gratification. Execution focuses on high-traffic campus areas to maximize engagement and minimize wait times, supporting operational efficiency and trend-driven product launches.

---

## Business Impact

Automated freezer vending streamlines labor costs, accelerates product trial, and expands reach to digitally engaged student markets. It enables rapid, limited-time launches and continuous demand capture without increasing frontline staff, supporting both operational savings and revenue growth.
