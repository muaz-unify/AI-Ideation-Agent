# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Eco-conscious young adults and millennials who prioritize transparency and sustainability in their food choices, frequenting premium retail stores and seeking products with verifiable environmental credentials.

---

## Value Proposition

General Mills offers chocolate bars produced entirely in solar-powered facilities, each featuring a traceable batch QR code. This code allows consumers to verify the bar's sustainability credentials instantly, providing transparency from production to purchase. The approach directly addresses rising consumer demand for environmentally friendly products, making it easy for shoppers to confirm the renewable energy source and ethical production behind every bar. The solution is operationally feasible for multi-brand rollout and supports clear, measurable success metrics by tracking QR code engagement and sustainable batch throughput.

---

## Business Impact

This initiative strengthens General Mills' sustainability leadership, increases brand preference among eco-driven shoppers, and enables precise measurement of consumer trust and engagement through QR code analytics. The model is scalable across brands, driving both revenue and reputational gains.
