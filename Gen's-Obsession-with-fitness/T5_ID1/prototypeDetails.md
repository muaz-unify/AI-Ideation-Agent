# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Adults enrolled in health insurance plans with wellness incentive programs, particularly those motivated by health outcomes and reward-based participation.

---

## Value Proposition

General Mills partners with leading health insurers to integrate select cereals and snacks into wellness incentive programs. Insured adults gain access to discounted or reward-eligible General Mills products by participating in approved healthy behaviors, such as regular check-ups or fitness milestones. This program leverages secure data integration to verify participation while minimizing administrative overhead. The partnership provides insurers with a tangible, value-added health engagement tool, while General Mills expands its reach to a broader, health-oriented consumer segment. Execution requires close collaboration on data privacy and reward logistics, with strategic intensity on seamless integration and operational scalability.

---

## Business Impact

This initiative drives incremental product volume by tapping into a new, health-engaged consumer base, boosts brand relevance in preventive health, and forges repeat purchase patterns through insurer-facilitated rewards. Business value is realized via increased distribution, higher brand equity, and sustained engagement.
