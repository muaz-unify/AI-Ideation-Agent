# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Hospital-based healthcare professionals (physicians, dietitians, and nursing staff) who participate in CME activities via digital health platforms and influence patient dietary recommendations.

---

## Value Proposition

General Mills offers CME-accredited webinars embedded within widely-used digital health platforms, allowing healthcare professionals to access credible, clinically relevant product education as part of their ongoing medical learning. These webinars are designed for seamless, risk-averse integration into hospital and clinic workflows—delivering targeted nutrition content that aligns with evolving professional engagement models. By prioritizing professional credibility and regulatory compliance, the initiative ensures that General Mills' products are positioned as trusted dietary solutions within the clinical decision-making environment, supporting the goal of being recognized as a clinically relevant dietary partner.

---

## Business Impact

This program will drive brand credibility and preference among healthcare professionals by embedding General Mills education into their daily clinical routines. The operational effect will be increased brand trust and product recommendation rates within hospitals and clinics, supporting measurable positioning as a relevant dietary partner.
