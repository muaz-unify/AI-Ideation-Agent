# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Busy young professionals aged 25–40 living in urban areas, seeking convenient, nutritious breakfast options that fit into fast-paced weekday routines and support active, health-conscious lifestyles.

---

## Value Proposition

General Mills introduces a line of chilled, ready-to-eat breakfast bowls featuring high-protein grains, Greek yogurt, and functional add-ins such as probiotics or fiber. Designed for grab-and-go convenience, these bowls deliver balanced nutrition without preparation time, directly addressing the needs of busy professionals. The product leverages cold chain logistics to ensure freshness and quality, aligning with the demand for functional, health-forward foods. Execution will focus on streamlined formulation and targeted distribution in urban retail and workplace channels, supporting the goal of providing accessible, nutritious breakfasts for time-pressed consumers.

---

## Business Impact

This initiative drives incremental revenue by capturing share in the growing market for convenient, high-protein breakfast solutions. It leverages General Mills' brand equity and cold chain capabilities to expand reach among urban professionals, supporting both trial and repeat purchase through differentiated product positioning.
