# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Digitally active young adults (ages 18–30) who prioritize health and nutrition, frequently engage with food trends on social media, and are open to trying new, protein-rich breakfast options.

---

## Value Proposition

General Mills will launch a targeted social media campaign showcasing its new protein-rich cereals, leveraging influencer partnerships and user-generated content to drive awareness and trial. The campaign will feature authentic, relatable moments of young adults enjoying the cereal in their daily routines, with interactive posts and stories designed to spark conversation and sharing. This approach ensures the product is positioned as both nutritious and culturally relevant, directly engaging the audience where they spend their time and aligning with their digital consumption habits.

---

## Business Impact

This initiative will generate immediate demand for the new protein-rich cereals by driving digital engagement and product trial among a high-potential segment. Increased social buzz and peer recommendations will accelerate sales velocity, support rapid retail sell-through, and strengthen brand relevance with younger consumers.
