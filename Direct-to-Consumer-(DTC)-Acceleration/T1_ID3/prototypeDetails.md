# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Urban and suburban families and young professionals who shop at neighborhood grocery and convenience stores, seeking accessible, trusted food brands and variety for daily meals and snacks.

---

## Value Proposition

General Mills will expand its multi-brand product range—including cereals, snacks, and baking mixes—into local retail markets and convenience shops. The initiative will focus on establishing strong in-store presence with prominent shelf placement, enabling consumers to easily find and purchase a variety of General Mills products during regular shopping trips. Execution will prioritize collaboration with local retailers to ensure displays meet both brand and store needs. The approach is multi-brand friendly, supporting omnichannel engagement and providing local customers with broader access to trusted products, directly supporting increased customer engagement and presence.

---

## Business Impact

Expanding into local retail shops will increase product visibility, drive impulse and planned purchases, and build brand loyalty within neighborhood markets. This broader distribution will generate incremental revenue and strengthen General Mills' position in key local segments.
