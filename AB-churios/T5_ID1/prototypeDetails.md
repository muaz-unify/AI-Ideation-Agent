# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Young parents with babies (ages 6–24 months) who seek convenient, nutritious, and engaging breakfast/snack options delivered directly to their homes and value playful, shareable experiences for their families.

---

## Value Proposition

General Mills will launch a monthly Churios variety subscription box, exclusively available via a direct-to-consumer (DTC) website. Each box features an assortment of Churios flavors tailored for babies and young families. The experience is enhanced by social media integration, encouraging parents to share unboxing moments and provide feedback directly on the platform. This approach increases brand engagement, captures real-time consumer insights, and leverages the growing trend of family-focused subscription products, aligning with operational feasibility and moderate logistics costs.

---

## Business Impact

The subscription model drives recurring revenue and deeper household penetration. Social sharing amplifies organic reach, while direct feedback loops improve product development and retention. The service differentiates Churios in the crowded baby breakfast/snack category, supporting the goal to increase focus on babies.
