# Marketing Brief

---

## Target Persona

Families with children aged 6–14 who enjoy hands-on food experiences and are active on social media, seeking creative, interactive snack options for home activities and sharing.

---

## Value Proposition

Churios DIY Snack Kits provide families with all the ingredients and step-by-step instructions needed to create unique Churios-based snacks at home. Each kit is designed for easy assembly and includes access to exclusive online recipe tutorials and unboxing content, encouraging creativity and family bonding. The kits are promoted through influencer-led unboxing videos and recipe demonstrations, leveraging social media trends to drive engagement. Packaging and kit contents are optimized for moderate cost and ease of use, ensuring a fun, mess-free experience that aligns with current hands-on, at-home activity trends.

---

## Business Impact

This initiative drives incremental revenue by tapping into the experiential snacking trend, increasing Churios brand engagement and household penetration. Social media-driven promotion amplifies reach, while limited-edition kits create urgency and repeat purchase opportunities.
